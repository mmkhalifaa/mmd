ii

iii

03Our posture

One
One platform, many surfaces. We build the agent workforce once, and dress it differently for each person who uses it.
Two
Agents are employees. Identity, entitlements, a supervisor, a log — from day one. That discipline is what lets us move fast.
Three
The human owns the relationship. Agents expand what an advisor can hold. They never change who the client calls.

i
Pillar one

Every advisor, amplified.

One text box, backed by everything behind it, and a day view that stays a step ahead. The advisor says what they want — an analysis, a plan, a trade check, an app that does not exist yet — and the platform answers, builds, or executes. No menus. No eleven systems.

“Watch Brian’s concentrated position. If it moves, draft the hedge and get me on the phone.” One sentence, and an agent is on the job — one of dozens each advisor runs like staff. This is how the best already think. Now it is how their tools work.

iPillar one · A day on the platform

What a Tuesday looks like.

The inbox agent worked the night shift: every request triaged, the routine ones executed and logged, the judgment calls drafted and queued for a decision over coffee.
Before the open, the day view is set — which clients to call, why now, what changed since the last conversation — with the brief already attached.
A question that used to be a week of analyst work — which clients are overexposed if rates hold and spreads widen — comes back in minutes, across every position and every document.
A client asks for something mid-conversation. The advisor asks the platform. A working tool — a scenario, a comparison, a plan — is on the screen before the call ends.
The autonomy dial
None of this is all-or-nothing. Every workflow starts human-approved. As an agent builds a record, low-risk actions graduate to straight-through — with the log to prove every step. Autonomy is earned, never assumed.

iiPillar two · Signal out

One specialist’s best idea, in front of every right client. Same day.

The idea
The CIO publishes a view — on gold, on rates, on a sector. Once. A broadcast agent takes it from there; the specialist never writes it twice.
Weeks → same day
From published view to client conversation
The translation
The agent turns the view into per-client actions: clients matching one profile see one move, clients matching another see a different one. Suitability and entitlements filter every match before anything lands in an advisor’s day view.
One view → every fitting client
Matched on profile, suitability, and mandate
The trail
This is advice at scale, and we treat it that way. The filter logic is reviewed and approved before it ever runs, and every recommendation traces back to its source.
Reviewable by design
View → filter → advisor → client, all logged
iii
Pillar three

Machinery that disappears.

An address change. A mortgage application. A line of credit. Account maintenance. Today each one is a ticket, a queue, and a wait. Next, an agent assembles the work end to end, and a person decides only where judgment matters.

Advisors stop waiting on operations. Clients stop waiting on the bank. And the capacity that comes back is what pays for everything else in this deck.

Pillar four

The human edge.

The bank does not get less human as the agents arrive. The human parts get sharper: new bankers train harder before they ever meet a real client, leaders see their market as it moves, and clients sit in rooms with capability they cannot get anywhere else.

Three builds carry it: the simulator, the tower, and the room.

ivPillar four · The bench and the tower

Grow the bench. Run the market.

The simulator
Clients who are not real
Every new analyst runs a synthetic book: AI clients with inboxes, voices, portfolios, and life events that respond like the real thing. They prospect, plan, and handle a market shock — scored and coached on every move — the way surgeons train on simulators before an operating room.
Synthetic clients → live scenarios → scoring → coaching → banker-ready in months, not years.
The tower
Leaders who see live
Coverage, pipeline, coaching signals, early warnings on client and talent risk — a live view of the market a leader runs, refreshed all day. The agents draft the intervention, not just the report.
Live market view → coaching signals → early warnings → drafted actions.

In every meeting room
Intelligence you cannot rent
When a client sits down with a banker, an AI joins the room — live analysis on the screen, drawing on the client’s full picture, the firm’s data, and the CIO’s current views. The banker drives; the client watches an idea become analysis in real time. Compute is cheap. This context is not.
The meeting nobody else can run.
Before anyone calls
Self-serve that respects the client
The everyday questions — answered instantly, at any hour, in the client’s own context. And a handoff to the human relationship at exactly the moment it matters, with the full thread attached.
The platform absorbs the routine. The relationship gets the interesting questions.
